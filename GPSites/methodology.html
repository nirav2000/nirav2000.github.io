Dataset methodology and limitations

This is an independently maintained research index of publicly listed GP organisations and publicly attributed supplier relationships. It is not affiliated with the NHS, CQC or the suppliers listed.

ODS identifiers identify organisations, not patients. This public dataset contains no patient information, private contact details, internal research notes, raw scans or credentials. Source files, detailed research and ingestion pipelines are held separately in a private repository.

Historic public evidence, technology-register associations, live supplier credits and current commercial contracts are different things. A supplier name in this directory must not be taken as proof of an active contract or a current service outage. Coverage counts and the last data refresh are displayed on the dashboard.

Source information: NHS Organisation Data Service (Open Government Licence, where applicable), CQC and NHS Tech Register (independent third-party register). Public supplier attributions are linked per organisation when available. Third-party sources may have separate licences and accuracy limitations.

Public data is intentionally minimised. This site has no account sign-in, tracking cookies, patient forms or analytics scripts.
