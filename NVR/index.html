A repeatable solving routine

1 · Separate

Look at shape, number, shading, position and direction one at a time.

2 · Test

Check your proposed rule against every step or pair, not just one.

3 · Eliminate

Rule out options that fail one feature. Check the remaining features.

4 · Explain

Say why your answer fits. On a fresh question, try the same method without help.

This is general technique guidance, not a diagnosis of why any particular answer was wrong.
